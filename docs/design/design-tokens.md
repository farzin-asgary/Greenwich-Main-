# Greenwich Design Tokens

## Colors
- **Greenwich Emerald**: `emerald-500` to `emerald-900`
- **Greenwich Gold**: `#d4af37`
- **Midnight**: `#0b1312`, `#121e1c`, `#1b4332`
- **Cream / Paper**: `emerald-50` or `#fbf9f5`

## Typography
- **Primary Persian Font**: `Vazirmatn`
- **Primary Latin Font**: `Playfair Display` (For editorial brand moments ONLY)
- **Monospace Font**: `Fira Code` / `Courier` (For mono uses)

## Spacing & Radius
- **sm**: `rounded-lg` (8px)
- **md**: `rounded-xl` (12px)
- **lg**: `rounded-2xl` (16px)
- **xl**: `rounded-3xl` (24px)
- **full**: `rounded-full`
